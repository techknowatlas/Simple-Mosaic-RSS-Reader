================================================================================
                          MOSAIC RSS READER v0.1.0
================================================================================

A beautiful desktop RSS feed reader with customizable mosaic tile interface
and stunning color themes.

================================================================================
INSTALLATION
================================================================================

Choose one of the installers:

1. MSI Installer (Recommended for Enterprise/Corporate):
   - Run: Mosaic RSS_0.1.0_x64_en-US.msi
   - Standard Windows Installer package
   - Suitable for Group Policy deployment

2. NSIS Setup (Recommended for General Use):
   - Run: Mosaic RSS_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe
   - User-friendly setup wizard
   - Most popular choice for individual users

Simply double-click your chosen installer and follow the on-screen instructions.

================================================================================
FEATURES
================================================================================

📰 RSS FEED MANAGEMENT
  - Add unlimited RSS/Atom feeds
  - Auto-refresh feed content
  - Quick-add popular feeds (Hacker News, TechCrunch, etc.)
  - Persistent storage - your feeds are saved between sessions

🎨 6 STUNNING COLOR THEMES
  - Emerald & Gold (Default) - Seafoam green with shiny gold borders
  - Ocean Blue & Silver - Calm blue tones with silver accents
  - Purple & Rose Gold - Rich purple with pink metallic borders
  - Sunset Orange & Bronze - Warm sunset colors with bronze
  - Ruby Red & Platinum - Bold red with elegant platinum
  - Midnight & Gold - Dark mode with golden highlights

✨ CUSTOMIZATION
  - Click the "🎨 Theme" button to change themes instantly
  - Themes affect tiles, headers, borders, and feed items
  - Your theme choice is automatically saved
  - Golden shimmer effects on hover
  - Smooth animations and transitions

📱 MOSAIC TILE INTERFACE
  - Drag and drop tiles to rearrange
  - Resize tiles to fit your preference
  - Click any article to open in your default browser
  - Refresh button on each tile for manual updates

💾 DATA STORAGE
  - All your feeds and preferences are saved locally
  - Data stored in: %APPDATA%\com.mosaicrss.dev\
  - No cloud sync - your data stays private on your computer

================================================================================
GETTING STARTED
================================================================================

1. Launch Mosaic RSS Reader
2. Click "Manage Feeds" in the header
3. Add your first RSS feed:
   - Enter feed URL (e.g., https://example.com/feed.xml)
   - Or use a Quick Add button for popular feeds
4. Click "🎨 Theme" to customize colors
5. Drag tiles to rearrange your layout
6. Click any article to read in your browser

================================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

Issue: App won't start
Solution: Make sure you have all Windows updates installed

Issue: Feeds not loading
Solution: Check your internet connection and verify the feed URL is correct

Issue: Theme not changing
Solution: Try restarting the application

Issue: Data not saving
Solution: Ensure the app has write permissions to %APPDATA%
