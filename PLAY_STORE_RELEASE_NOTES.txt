📰 Mosaic RSS Reader - Your News, Your Way

Tired of cluttered news apps? Mosaic RSS Reader brings a fresh approach to staying informed. Organize your favorite news sources, blogs, and market data in a beautiful, customizable mosaic tile layout.

✨ KEY FEATURES

RSS Feed Management
• Add unlimited RSS and Atom feeds
• Auto-refresh to get the latest content
• Clean, distraction-free reading
• One-tap to open articles in browser

Market Data Tracking
• Track stocks, indices, and crypto
• Pre-configured watchlists (S&P 500, Nasdaq, Dow Jones, etc.)
• Real-time price quotes and changes
• Mix market data with news feeds

Beautiful Mosaic Interface
• Arrange tiles your way
• Responsive design for any screen size
• Touch-optimized for mobile
• Smooth scrolling experience

Personalization
• Customizable themes and colors
• Control refresh intervals (5-60 min)
• Your layout saves automatically
• Fast, local data storage

🚀 WHY MOSAIC RSS?

Unlike traditional RSS readers, Mosaic lets you combine news feeds and market data in one unified view. Perfect for investors, traders, and news enthusiasts who want everything in one place.

📱 MODERN & FAST

Built with cutting-edge technology (Rust + React) for native performance. Efficient memory usage and smart caching mean fast startup times and smooth operation.

🛡️ PRIVACY FOCUSED

• No tracking or analytics
• Your data stays on your device
• No account required
• Open source friendly

🎯 PERFECT FOR

• News junkies who follow multiple sources
• Investors tracking markets and financial news
• Professionals monitoring industry updates
• Anyone who loves RSS feeds

⚡ QUICK START

1. Tap "Manage Feeds" to add your favorite RSS feeds
2. Tap "Market Watch" to track stocks and indices
3. Customize your layout and enjoy!

Popular feeds to try:
• NY Times, The Guardian, BBC News
• TechCrunch, The Verge, Hacker News
• Yahoo Finance, MarketWatch

🔧 TECHNICAL SPECS

• Android 7.0+ required
• ~40MB storage
• Internet connection needed
• Supports RSS 2.0 and Atom feeds
